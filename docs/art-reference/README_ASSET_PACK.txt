Postmark 250 Asset Pack
=========================

Contents:

individual_stamps_high_res/
  12 final stamp PNGs cropped from the two approved 6-stamp sheets.
  These are opaque crops to preserve dark artwork inside the stamps.

individual_stamps_from_collector/
  12 individual crops from the final collector sheet, including parchment around each stamp.

stamp_sheets/
  The two approved 6-stamp sheets.

collector_sheet/
  The full combined Postmark 250 collector sheet.

background_layers/
  postmark_250_blank_declaration_background.png
    The Declaration-style collector background with stamp areas blanked out.

  postmark_250_frame_with_transparent_stamp_slots.png
    A transparent-slot frame/overlay version for compositing.

metadata/
  layout_metadata.json
    Pixel coordinates for the 12 stamp slots on the collector sheet.

  individual_stamp_preview.jpg
    Quick preview sheet of all 12 high-res individual crops.
